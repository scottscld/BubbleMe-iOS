BUBBLE ME — OPEN THIS FIRST
===========================

This folder is a finished Xcode project. You do NOT create a new project
in Xcode. You just open this one and press Play.

You need:
  • A Mac (Xcode does not run on Windows or iPad)
  • Xcode from the Mac App Store (search “Xcode”, install, open it once
    so it finishes installing extra tools)


STEP 1 — Unzip
--------------
If this is still a .zip file:
  1. Double-click the zip
  2. A folder appears (BubbleMe-Xcode or BubbleMe-iOS-main)
  3. Open that folder


STEP 2 — Open the project
-------------------------
Double-click the file named:

    BubbleMe.xcodeproj

It has a blue Xcode icon. That is the project. Do not open the individual
.swift files by themselves.

If your Mac says “Are you sure you want to open it?” click Open.


STEP 3 — Pick a phone
---------------------
At the TOP CENTER of Xcode you will see something like:

    BubbleMe    >    iPhone 16

Tap the phone name and choose any iPhone simulator
(iPhone 16, iPhone 15, iPhone 14 — any of them is fine).


STEP 4 — Press Play
-------------------
Click the triangle Play button at the top left
(or press the Command key and the R key together).

The first time takes 1–3 minutes. A fake iPhone window (the Simulator)
will open. Tap Play Arcade, then drag to aim and release to shoot.


THAT’S IT
---------
You should see:
  • Home — Arcade, today’s Daily Board (1 of 90), Continue, streak
  • Levels — 500 stages with 1–3 stars (25 / 50 / 75 Bubbles)
  • Battle — CPU that shoots back, or a 4-letter friend room
  • Bomb / Fire / Face in the bag. Battle also has ZigZag / Mirror / Shuffle
  • Pause saves. Continue on Home resumes. Music + SFX in Settings


IF XCODE ASKS ABOUT A TEAM (signing)
------------------------------------
1. In the LEFT sidebar click the blue BubbleMe icon at the very top
2. Click the BubbleMe target under TARGETS
3. Open the “Signing & Capabilities” tab
4. Check “Automatically manage signing”
5. Team: click Add Account… and sign in with your Apple ID (free)

The Simulator works even with Team set to None. A real iPhone needs the
Apple ID step.


TO RUN ON YOUR REAL IPHONE
--------------------------
1. Plug the iPhone in with a cable
2. Unlock the phone and tap Trust if asked
3. In Xcode, pick your iPhone from the device list (instead of a simulator)
4. Press Play
5. On the iPhone go to Settings → General → VPN & Device Management
   and trust your Apple ID

A free Apple ID install lasts 7 days, then press Play again in Xcode.
TestFlight needs a paid Apple Developer account — you do not need that.


WHAT THE BUTTONS DO IN THE GAME
-------------------------------
Play Arcade  — endless high score. Same boards all week. Resets Saturday
               midnight Chicago time. Unlimited shots.
Daily Board  — 90 unique boards, one per calendar day (Chicago).
Levels       — 500 unique stages. Stars: 1 = 25 Bubbles, 2 = 50, 3 = 75
               (you only get the difference if you beat a previous star).
Continue     — resume a paused run (you can leave to Battle or Home).
Battle CPU   — same board; CPU shoots on its copy and uses battle powers
               on you. ZigZag / Mirror / Shuffle hit the opponent.
Friend room  — Create a 4-letter code, share it. Friend taps Join. Nearby
               Wi-Fi / Bluetooth. If nobody connects, a stand-in plays.
Pause        — saves progress. Home / Battle / quit are all safe.
Me           — Game Center sign-in syncs Bubbles, cosmetics, scores.
Settings     — Music, SFX, haptics, alerts.
Friends      — 6-letter friend code. Messages / Email / More.


POWER-UPS (same as bubbleme.fun)
--------------------------------
Arcade / Levels / Daily:
  Bomb  — pops a radius around the landing spot
  Fire  — melts a line of bubbles
  Face  — wipes every bubble of the color you hit

Battle only (used on the opponent):
  ZigZag  — their next shots swerve
  Mirror  — their aim is flipped
  Shuffle — their board colors scramble


COMMON PROBLEMS
---------------
“Untrusted developer” on iPhone
    Settings → General → VPN & Device Management → tap your name → Trust.

White screen / blank game
    Make sure you opened BubbleMe.xcodeproj, not a single .swift file.
    Product menu → Clean Build Folder, then Play again.

No vibration / no Game Center
    The Simulator cannot vibrate. Use a real iPhone.
    Game Center sign-in may ask you to sign into iCloud on the device.

No music
    Settings → Music on. Turn the iPhone silent switch off.

Build failed, red errors
    Xcode menu → Settings → Platforms. Install iOS if it asks.
    Quit Xcode, open BubbleMe.xcodeproj again, press Play.

“iPhone is busy” / “Preparing…”
    Wait. The first install on a phone can take a few minutes.

Friend battle “can’t find them”
    Both phones on the same Wi-Fi. Allow local network when iOS asks.
    Or tap Play a stand-in.

You do not need to add SpriteKit, copy files, or edit Info.plist.
That is already done inside this project.
